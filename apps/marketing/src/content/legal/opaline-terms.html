Schedule 1 — Processing Details

1. Subject matter

Provision of hosted coding-agent session storage, display, analytics and workspace-management functionality.

2. Duration

For the term of the agreement and any limited period afterward required to return, delete or lawfully retain Customer Personal Data.

3. Nature of processing

The processing operations include:

  • collection and receipt;
  • recording;
  • validation;
  • organisation and structuring;
  • parsing;
  • storage;
  • retrieval;
  • consultation and display;
  • analysis;
  • extraction of metadata and metrics;
  • generation of Workspace Analytics;
  • association with Users, projects and workspaces;
  • access-control enforcement;
  • transmission to authorised subprocessors;
  • backup, where applicable;
  • export;
  • restriction;
  • deletion; and
  • destruction.

4. Purpose

The purpose is to provide the Hosted Service to the Customer, including:

  • session ingestion and storage;
  • transcript display;
  • token and usage analytics;
  • workflow and interaction analytics;
  • User-, project- and workspace-level reporting;
  • session classifications and scores;
  • access and permission management;
  • Customer-requested support;
  • security and abuse prevention; and
  • deletion and export functionality.

Opaline does not process substantive Session Data for advertising, AI-model training, independent research or cross-customer benchmarking.

5. Categories of data subjects

Customer Personal Data may relate to:

  • the Customer;
  • Authorised Users;
  • employees;
  • contractors;
  • consultants;
  • developers;
  • administrators;
  • the Customer's clients and end users;
  • individuals communicating with the Customer; and
  • other persons whose personal data appear in Session Data.

6. Categories of personal data

Customer Personal Data can include:

  • names;
  • usernames;
  • email addresses;
  • account and organisation identifiers;
  • project and repository information;
  • source code;
  • file names, paths and contents;
  • prompts and model responses;
  • system and agent messages;
  • terminal commands and output;
  • tool calls, inputs and results;
  • URLs;
  • commit, branch and remote information;
  • timestamps;
  • interaction and usage records;
  • token and model information;
  • skill, command and sub-agent information;
  • classifications and scores;
  • technical identifiers;
  • business and professional information;
  • communications;
  • confidential information;
  • credentials or secrets inadvertently appearing in Session Data; and
  • any other personal data submitted within or derived from a coding-agent session.

7. Special categories and highly sensitive data

The Hosted Service is not intended for intentional processing of:

  • special-category personal data under Article 9 GDPR;
  • criminal-offence data under Article 10 GDPR;
  • medical records;
  • payment-card information;
  • government identification documents; or
  • credentials and private keys.

Such data may nevertheless appear incidentally in complete Session Data.

The Customer must not intentionally submit such data unless Opaline has expressly agreed in writing and appropriate safeguards have been established.

8. Processing frequency

Processing occurs whenever the Customer or an Authorised User:

  • uploads a session;
  • enables an upload hook;
  • accesses or filters a dashboard;
  • views a transcript;
  • changes workspace permissions;
  • creates a public share;
  • requests support; or
  • submits a deletion or export instruction.

Session-upload hooks may cause recurring submissions while enabled.

9. Retention

Customer Personal Data are retained until:

  • the relevant session is deleted;
  • the relevant account or workspace is deleted;
  • the Customer terminates the agreement; or
  • another lawful instruction requires deletion.

To the extent backup copies are maintained, residual copies may remain only until overwritten through the relevant provider's ordinary backup cycle.

Public-share snapshots remain publicly available until deleted or replaced, or until they automatically expire after 30 days.

10. Instructions

The Customer's instructions consist of:

  • these Terms and this DPA;
  • configuration within the Hosted Service;
  • uploads and API calls;
  • settings selected by Authorised Users;
  • role and permission assignments;
  • support communications;
  • deletion and export requests; and
  • other written instructions accepted by Opaline.

Schedule 2 — Technical and Organisational Measures

Opaline maintains the following measures, taking account of the nature and risks of the Hosted Service.

1. Governance and confidentiality

  • Access to Customer Personal Data is restricted to persons who require access for authorised operational purposes.
  • Persons authorised to process personal data are subject to confidentiality obligations.
  • Customer Personal Data are not routinely inspected for purposes unrelated to providing or securing the Hosted Service.
  • Data-protection and security responsibilities are assigned to the operator of Opaline.

2. Authentication and account security

  • Authenticated access is required for private workspaces.
  • Authentication uses supported email, OAuth, bearer-token or API-key mechanisms.
  • API keys can be revoked.
  • Sessions and account credentials are protected through access-control mechanisms.
  • Customers control membership, roles and permissions within their organisations.

3. Authorisation and tenant separation

  • Requests for private Customer data are subject to organisation and membership checks.
  • Customer data are associated with organisation and User identifiers.
  • Access permissions restrict ordinary Users from accessing unauthorised raw sessions.
  • Administrative and service access is limited according to operational need.
  • Public access is limited to snapshots intentionally created through public-share functionality.

4. Transmission security

  • Hosted web and API traffic is transmitted through HTTPS/TLS.
  • Authentication credentials are not intentionally transmitted in plaintext.
  • Connections to infrastructure providers use secured interfaces made available by those providers.

5. Infrastructure security

  • The application is hosted in managed infrastructure with physical and environmental security handled by the relevant infrastructure provider.
  • Production infrastructure is separated from local development environments.
  • Service origins and cross-origin access are restricted through configured trusted origins and access controls.
  • Request-size and rate limits are used to reduce abuse and operational risk.

6. Availability and resilience

  • Opaline uses managed hosting and database infrastructure designed for service availability.
  • Backup and recovery capabilities can differ between the relevant data stores and infrastructure providers.
  • Health checks and service monitoring are used to detect availability problems.
  • Opaline maintains procedures for responding to operational failures.
  • Opaline does not provide a contractual backup or disaster-recovery service unless expressly agreed in writing.

7. Data minimisation and purpose limitation

  • Product Analytics are designed not to include raw prompts, model responses, source code, file contents or terminal output.
  • Session Data are processed only for the Customer workspace for which they were submitted.
  • Opaline does not combine substantive Session Data across unrelated Customers.
  • Access to Customer Personal Data is limited to the purposes described in this DPA.

8. Logging and monitoring

  • Application requests and operational errors are logged for security, troubleshooting and service operation.
  • Authentication, access and upload-status information can be processed for security and abuse prevention.
  • Logs are protected through infrastructure access controls.
  • Opaline reviews relevant logs where necessary to investigate incidents or service failures.

9. Incident response

  • Opaline investigates suspected security incidents affecting the Hosted Service.
  • Opaline takes reasonable containment and remediation measures.
  • Personal-data breaches affecting Customer Personal Data are notified under Clause 11.
  • Opaline cooperates with affected Customers as required by Applicable Data Protection Law.

10. Deletion and account controls

  • Customers can delete accounts and organisations through available service functionality.
  • Deletion operations remove associated data from active application and analytics databases.
  • API credentials associated with deleted accounts are disabled or removed.
  • Where backup copies exist, they remain protected and expire through the relevant provider's ordinary backup cycle.
  • Deletion instructions are reapplied if a backup containing deleted data is restored.

11. Subprocessor management

  • Opaline assesses subprocessors relevant to Customer Personal Data.
  • Subprocessors are bound through written data-protection obligations.
  • International transfers are protected through an applicable Chapter V mechanism.
  • Access by subprocessors is limited to the service they provide.

12. Review and improvement

  • Opaline may update technical and organisational measures as the service and risks evolve.
  • Updates must not materially reduce the overall protection of Customer Personal Data.
  • Security vulnerabilities and dependencies are addressed on a risk-based basis.
