2. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to the hosted Opaline service, including:

  • opaline.so;
  • Opaline's hosted API;
  • the Opaline command-line interface when connected to the hosted API; and
  • related hosted features and integrations.

Opaline is also available as open-source software. This Privacy Policy does not apply to independently operated self-hosted installations.

Opaline does not receive or control information that remains exclusively within a self-hosted installation. Where a self-hosted installation is configured to transmit information to Opaline's hosted services, this Privacy Policy applies only to the information transmitted to Opaline.

The hosted service is intended for business and independent professional use and is not directed at persons under 18 years of age.

3. Data-protection roles

Session Data and Workspace Analytics

Customers determine:

  • which coding-agent sessions are uploaded;
  • which Users, projects and repositories are connected;
  • who can access a workspace;
  • why Session Data are processed; and
  • how the resulting analytics are used.

For personal data contained in uploaded sessions and analytics derived from those sessions:

  • the Customer is the controller; and
  • Opaline acts as processor on the Customer's behalf.

Opaline processes Session Data and Workspace Analytics only:

  • on the Customer's documented instructions;
  • to provide the hosted service to that Customer;
  • under the applicable Data Processing Agreement; and
  • where required by law.

Opaline does not determine an independent purpose for Session Data or Workspace Analytics.

Opaline's own processing

Opaline acts as controller for processing relating to:

  • account creation and administration;
  • authentication;
  • organisation and access management;
  • operation and security of the service;
  • support and operational communications;
  • Product Analytics; and
  • compliance with legal obligations and the handling of legal claims.

4. Information Opaline processes

Account and organisation information

Opaline processes:

  • name;
  • email address;
  • profile image;
  • User, account and organisation identifiers;
  • organisation memberships and roles;
  • invitation information;
  • authentication provider;
  • authentication and login records;
  • account settings and preferences; and
  • API and CLI credentials in protected form.

Users can authenticate through email verification and, where available, Google or GitHub.

Google and GitHub process information for their own authentication services under their respective privacy notices.

Coding-agent Session Data

Opaline is designed to receive complete sessions generated by supported coding agents.

Uploaded Session Data can include:

  • user prompts and model responses;
  • system, assistant and agent messages;
  • source code;
  • file names, paths and contents;
  • project and package information;
  • terminal commands and command output;
  • tool calls, inputs and results;
  • standard output and error output;
  • repository, remote, branch and commit information;
  • URLs and references to external resources;
  • timestamps and session identifiers;
  • token and model information;
  • skill, slash-command and sub-agent activity;
  • personal data relating to Users or third parties;
  • confidential business information and trade secrets; and
  • credentials, API keys or access tokens that appeared during a session.

Because complete transcripts are uploaded, Customers and Users should ensure that they are authorised to process and provide the information contained in them.

Workspace Analytics

On the Customer's instructions, Opaline parses uploaded Session Data to create analytics for the Customer's workspace.

These analytics include:

  • session dates and duration;
  • interaction counts and timing;
  • token usage;
  • model usage;
  • repository and project information;
  • skill, slash-command and sub-agent usage;
  • error indicators;
  • plan-mode and commit indicators;
  • response-time and pause statistics;
  • session classifications or archetypes;
  • success or effectiveness scores; and
  • User-level, project-level and aggregate comparisons.

Workspace Analytics can relate to identifiable Users and may be visible to authorised organisation administrators, depending on workspace permissions.

Opaline does not use Session Data or Workspace Analytics to evaluate Users for Opaline's own purposes.

Technical and security information

Opaline processes technical information including:

  • IP address;
  • request time and requested resource;
  • browser and operating-system information;
  • application and CLI version;
  • API and upload status;
  • error codes;
  • authentication and access records;
  • request identifiers; and
  • information required to detect abuse or investigate security incidents.

Support communications

When a person contacts Opaline or uses the support widget, Opaline processes:

  • their name and contact information;
  • the contents of their request;
  • files or screenshots they submit;
  • account and organisation identifiers; and
  • technical information necessary to investigate and respond.

Opaline uses Chatwoot to provide the support widget and manage support conversations. Information submitted through the support widget is transmitted to and processed by Chatwoot in the United States.

Session content is accessed for support only when the affected Customer requests it and the issue cannot reasonably be resolved without that access.

5. How Opaline uses information

Providing accounts and access

Opaline processes Account Data to:

  • create and administer accounts;
  • authenticate Users;
  • manage organisations, invitations and permissions;
  • provide access to the hosted service; and
  • send necessary operational communications.

For Users who enter into an agreement with Opaline, the legal basis is Article 6(1)(b) GDPR.

For employees, contractors and other persons invited into a Customer's workspace, the legal basis is Article 6(1)(f) GDPR. Opaline's legitimate interest is administering the Customer's workspace and securely providing access to authorised Users.

Processing Session Data for Customers

Opaline processes Session Data and Workspace Analytics to:

  • receive and validate uploaded sessions;
  • parse, structure and store sessions;
  • retrieve and display sessions;
  • calculate usage, timing and workflow analytics;
  • associate sessions with the relevant User, project and workspace;
  • provide analytics and comparisons to authorised Users;
  • create optional public snapshots at a User's request;
  • maintain operational and backup copies; and
  • carry out deletion instructions.

Opaline performs these operations solely to provide the service to the relevant Customer.

Opaline does not:

  • sell Session Data or Workspace Analytics;
  • use them for advertising;
  • use them to train artificial-intelligence models;
  • combine them across unrelated Customers;
  • use them for independent product research;
  • use transcript content to improve Opaline for other Customers;
  • create cross-customer benchmarks; or
  • disclose them publicly unless an authorised User creates a public share.

Operating and securing Opaline

Opaline processes technical and security information to:

  • deliver the website, application and API;
  • maintain availability and stability;
  • diagnose technical failures;
  • protect accounts and infrastructure;
  • detect abuse and unauthorised access; and
  • establish, exercise or defend legal claims.

The legal basis is Article 6(1)(f) GDPR. Opaline's legitimate interests are operating and securing the service and protecting Customers and Users.

Support

Processing necessary to handle contractual or pre-contractual support requests is based on Article 6(1)(b) GDPR.

Other support processing is based on Article 6(1)(f) GDPR and Opaline's legitimate interest in responding to enquiries and resolving technical problems.

Product Analytics

Opaline uses PostHog to understand how Opaline's account, authentication, CLI, upload, dashboard and organisation workflows are used and to improve the usability, reliability and operation of the service.

PostHog Product Analytics are activated automatically when the relevant Opaline application, website, CLI or hook is used. Opaline does not currently request prior consent before Product Analytics begin.

Opaline uses PostHog's United States cloud region in Virginia. Product Analytics are therefore transmitted to and processed in the United States.

Depending on the event, Product Analytics can include:

  • User and organisation identifiers;
  • User name and email address where associated with an account;
  • pages and dashboards viewed;
  • authentication and onboarding actions;
  • upload success or failure;
  • upload method and client surface;
  • application and CLI version;
  • operating-system category;
  • organisation-management actions;
  • navigation, filters and utility interactions;
  • normalised error codes;
  • a persistent CLI installation identifier;
  • a hashed project identifier; and
  • broad content-size categories.

Product Analytics do not include raw Session Data.

Opaline does not transmit prompts, model responses, source code, file contents, terminal output or transcript tool inputs and results to PostHog.

Opaline does not use blanket click autocapture, session replay or PostHog surveys.

Opaline processes Product Analytics on the basis of Article 6(1)(f) GDPR. Opaline's legitimate interests are:

  • understanding whether important workflows function correctly;
  • diagnosing failures;
  • measuring product adoption and reliability;
  • improving the usability of the service; and
  • prioritising product development.

Users have the right to object to this processing at any time on grounds relating to their particular situation by contacting evren@opaline.so.

Where PostHog stores information on or accesses information from a User's browser, computer or other terminal device, that storage or access may separately be subject to § 25 TDDDG.

6. Cookies, local storage and device information

Opaline uses cookies, local storage and comparable technologies to:

  • maintain authenticated sessions;
  • protect against cross-site request forgery;
  • secure accounts;
  • provide requested functionality; and
  • support Product Analytics.

Technologies that are strictly necessary to provide functionality requested by the User are used under § 25(2) No. 2 TDDDG.

PostHog can store or access analytics identifiers and related information through:

  • browser cookies or browser storage;
  • local files used by the Opaline CLI;
  • persistent installation identifiers; and
  • Opaline hooks running on a User's device.

Opaline currently activates these Product Analytics technologies without obtaining prior consent.

The subsequent processing of personal data for Product Analytics is based on Article 6(1)(f) GDPR as described in Section 5.

7. Public shares

A User can choose to create a public Opaline "Wrapped" snapshot containing selected profile information and Workspace Analytics.

Public shares:

  • are accessible to anyone who has the public link;
  • do not require authentication;
  • contain a fixed snapshot rather than access to the Customer's private workspace; and
  • expire after 30 days unless refreshed or removed earlier.

Opaline creates a public share only following an authorised User's request.

8. Human access and encryption

Session Data are encrypted in transit through HTTPS/TLS.

Session Data are not end-to-end encrypted or zero-knowledge encrypted. They are technically accessible within the systems used to operate Opaline.

Opaline does not routinely read or inspect Session Data.

Access is limited to circumstances where it is reasonably necessary to:

  • handle a support request for which the Customer requested content access;
  • investigate a security incident or suspected abuse;
  • restore or protect the availability or integrity of the service;
  • diagnose a failure that cannot reasonably be investigated using metadata; or
  • comply with a binding legal obligation.

Access is limited to authorised persons and to the information necessary for the relevant purpose.

9. Storage, retention and deletion

Account Data

Account Data are retained while the relevant account remains active.

When a User deletes their account, Opaline deletes the User's account records, API credentials and associated Session Data from active systems without undue delay, unless particular information must be retained to comply with law, investigate abuse or handle legal claims.

Session Data and Workspace Analytics

Session Data and associated Workspace Analytics are retained until:

  • the relevant session is deleted;
  • the User's account is deleted;
  • the relevant organisation or workspace is deleted; or
  • the service relationship ends.

Deletion removes the relevant raw Session Data and associated Workspace Analytics from active databases.

Residual copies can remain temporarily in rolling backups until those backups are overwritten. Backup copies are not used for ordinary processing. If a backup is restored, existing deletion instructions are reapplied.

Public shares

Public shares expire after 30 days unless refreshed or deleted earlier.

Technical and security information

Technical and security information is retained only for as long as necessary to operate and secure the service, investigate incidents and defend legal claims.

Product Analytics

Product Analytics are retained according to Opaline's configured PostHog retention period and are deleted or anonymised when no longer required for product measurement and improvement.

Support communications

Support communications are retained for as long as necessary to resolve and document the request. They may be retained longer where required for an ongoing incident, legal obligation or legal claim.

11. International transfers

Opaline transfers personal data to service providers located in the United States, including PostHog, Chatwoot and Resend. Google and GitHub may also process personal data in the United States.

Personal data processed in the United States can be subject to lawful access by United States authorities.

Where a recipient is validly certified under the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework, transfers can be based on the European Commission's adequacy decision under Article 45 GDPR.

Where the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework does not apply, Opaline relies on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses under Article 46(2)(c) GDPR and, where required, supplementary technical, contractual or organisational safeguards.

Information about the transfer mechanism applicable to a particular provider can be requested through evren@opaline.so.

12. Security

Opaline uses technical and organisational safeguards intended to protect personal data and Session Data, including:

  • encrypted transmission through HTTPS/TLS;
  • authenticated access;
  • API-key and bearer-token authentication;
  • organisation and workspace access controls;
  • restrictions on access to other Users' raw sessions;
  • rate limiting;
  • token revocation;
  • separation of Customer data through organisation identifiers and permission checks; and
  • restricted administrative access.

No hosted service can guarantee absolute security.

Users should protect their devices and account credentials and promptly notify Opaline of suspected unauthorised access.

13. Automated processing and profiling

Opaline generates classifications, analytics and success or effectiveness scores from Session Data on the Customer's instructions.

Where these analytics evaluate an identifiable User's work activity or behaviour, they can constitute profiling under Article 4 No. 4 GDPR.

Opaline does not use these results for its own employment or personnel decisions and does not make decisions based solely on automated processing that produce legal or similarly significant effects concerning Users.

The Customer determines whether and how Workspace Analytics are used in decisions affecting its Users.

  • access their personal data;
  • correct inaccurate personal data;
  • request deletion;
  • request restriction of processing;
  • receive portable data;
  • object to processing based on legitimate interests; and
  • withdraw any consent previously given with effect for the future.

Requests concerning processing for which Opaline is controller can be sent to:

evren@opaline.so

For personal data contained in Session Data or Workspace Analytics, the relevant Customer is the controller. Requests should normally be directed to that Customer.

Following an objection, Opaline will stop the relevant processing unless Opaline demonstrates compelling legitimate grounds that override the data subject's interests, rights and freedoms, or the processing is necessary for legal claims.

Objections can be submitted to evren@opaline.so.
